Carbon Box Model
02-120 final project · mechanistic carbon cycle

Where does our CO₂ go?

This model follows carbon between four boxes: the air, the surface ocean, the deep ocean and land plants. Every flow follows a physical rule, such as ocean chemistry, mixing or plant growth. Nothing is fitted to the measured CO₂, so the CO₂ record is a fair test of the model.

What the tests show

  • Test 1 (1959 rates) passes the pulse test, but overshoots 2024 CO₂ by 48 ppm. Its ocean takes up about a third of the measured amount.
  • Changing one rate at a time shows that deep-ocean mixing controls ocean uptake. Air–sea exchange and chemistry barely matter.
  • Test 2 sets mixing from a measured ocean carbon inventory and plant growth from the land sink. It then predicts 2024 CO₂ within about 5 ppm, without using the CO₂ record.

Limits

  • Two ocean layers: one mixing rate can't match both the 1994 inventory and today's ocean sink.
  • The Global Carbon Budget itself leaves 80 GtC (38 ppm) unaccounted for since 1850.
  • No warming feedbacks, and a simple land model.
